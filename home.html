What Is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial Intelligence (AI) refers to computer systems designed to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence. These tasks include learning, reasoning, problem-solving, perception, and understanding language.

AI works by processing large amounts of data, identifying patterns, and making decisions based on those patterns. Unlike simple programming, AI systems can adapt and improve over time.

The field includes Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and Generative AI — technologies that power voice assistants, self-driving cars, chatbots, recommendation systems, and advanced data analysis tools.
